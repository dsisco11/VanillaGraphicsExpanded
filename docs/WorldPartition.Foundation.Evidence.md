## Scope and ownership

The new foundation lives directly in `WorldPartition`, the approved steady-state directory. The old `LumOn/WorldCells` registry and scene schedulers continue to own their existing cells until task 4 introduces their adapters. Their old identity and center contracts are not used by new registrations. This avoids assigning two owners to existing cell lifecycles. Local geometry wiring, actual GPU ring publication, game-world source adapters, diagnostic views, final budget values and live measurements remain in tasks 2–5.

A coordinator is constructed and pumped on the render thread, which is also its state/capture owner. Workers receive immutable request/snapshot contracts and may only enqueue immutable completion records. Providers use existing executors. Publication and retirement acknowledgements are synchronous on that owning thread; a backend with delayed physical deletion must retain ownership until safe reuse. Immutable provider payload interfaces require implementations to provide immutable backing data. Source domains must be bounded and practical to enumerate; this coordinator represents requested cells explicitly, not an unbounded spatial database.

A registration supplies its layout, fixed coverage margins/retention, provider and resource ceilings. Source priority and distance rank work within the required/speculative tiers. Cached source integer ranges apply only entering/leaving slabs; overlapping cells keep their incarnation and content. Spatial retention is checked only for the retained fringe, and its clock starts when a cell leaves requested coverage.

Cancellation is advisory. Dispatched work continues to consume in-flight credit until a matching completion arrives, even after cancellation or world teardown. Providers must acknowledge cancelled work. Capture-to-publication requests count against that same credit; speculative captured/completed work yields credit to newly required work. A feasible upload that cannot fit the remaining frame budget reserves subsequent service to prevent smaller uploads starving it. Required work can preempt speculative reservations.

An upload larger than an entire configured budget is parked as `BudgetBlocked`; its payload and in-flight credit are released, while desired demand and the byte shortfall remain observable. Explicit dirtying retries changed content; changing resource configuration uses a new registration. Required residency exceeding physical capacity is reported without deleting demand or repeatedly evicting required cells. Fair service applies to schedulable work; insufficient resident capacity cannot guarantee all required cells become ready. Final capacities and performance measurements remain later work.
